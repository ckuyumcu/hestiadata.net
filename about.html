HESTIA Data / About & project scope

What HESTIA Data does

HESTIA Data is an Ankara-based data venture offering real-world data collection and field services in Türkiye for teams working from abroad. Its public service areas are egocentric household video, field photo and video capture, document retrieval, local market evidence and supplier verification, plus small technical task recording pilots for Physical AI teams, subject to project feasibility.

These are service offerings, not evidence of completed customer projects or a claim that a dataset is available for immediate purchase. Availability and deliverables are confirmed for each project in a written quote.

Locations and project feasibility

Coordination is based in Ankara. We assess project requests throughout Türkiye, Northern Cyprus and Georgia, subject to local access and permissions, technical needs, suitable people and environments, and logistics. A regional enquiry is not a guarantee of a ready local team, site or delivery capability. Feasibility, responsibilities, coverage, price and proposed dates must be confirmed for each project in a written proposal.

The project FAQ explains briefs, pilots, rights, review, logistics and commercial arrangements.

Three service areas

AI training data collection concerns first-person recordings of household tasks. A brief needs to identify the tasks, target volume, collection conditions, review criteria and delivery requirements. Format and equipment requirements must be confirmed in the project scope.

Field services concern evidence from selected locations, including photos, records and supplier visits. Location, access permission and the evidence needed must be agreed before fieldwork. A visit can document observations at a place and time; it does not establish every fact about a company.

Technical task recording pilots for Physical AI start with the technical tasks and capture specification you define. We assess and organise suitable people and a working environment in Türkiye, review the required skills, technical expertise, equipment, safety responsibilities, participant permissions and data rights, then agree a small pilot's scope, output format, acceptance conditions and price. A task-specific fixture or physical setup can be considered as supporting work when needed, subject to feasibility. Industrial camera setup or integration is considered separately only with a concrete factory need, an identified budget and a suitably qualified technical partner; scope and responsibilities must be confirmed before commitment.

How an engagement starts

  1. Share the service needed, task definition and required output. For a recording pilot, include your capture specification.
  2. Review feasibility, suitable people and environment, technical requirements and permissions.
  3. Agree the scope, output format, acceptance conditions, price and proposed dates in writing before work begins.
  4. Review the small pilot or other agreed deliverable against the acceptance criteria.

The engagement workflow and brief form are on the main site. The form prepares an email draft for you to review and send; it is not a completed order.

Limits and personal data

HESTIA Data does not promise access to a location or record without the required permissions. Service descriptions do not establish legal certification, regulatory approval or a model-performance result. Project requirements, permitted uses and handling of personal data need to be settled before collection.

Read the privacy policy and terms of service for the published conditions. This explanation does not change either policy.

Contracting party

HESTIA Data is a service brand. Before an engagement, the written quote must name the legal entity responsible for the agreement, invoice and payment, together with the delivery owner. Do not infer the contracting party from a group or product brand.
