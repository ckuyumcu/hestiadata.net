Which projects does HESTIA Data cover?

HESTIA Data handles requests for engineering records from public- and private-sector construction projects, power plants and industrial facilities in Türkiye. Power projects can include solar, wind and hydroelectric plants. Availability depends on the project, the documents requested and the rights holder’s written permission.

Which engineering documents can I request?

For construction, examples include approved drawings, technical queries, approved revisions and acceptance records. For a power plant, buyers can request approved project drawings, site revisions, commissioning records and acceptance records. For an industrial facility, examples include technical specifications, maintenance work orders, inspection reports and quality test reports.

How does HESTIA obtain rights to the requested records?

HESTIA can supply the agreed records after obtaining a written licence from the relevant public authority or private rights holder for that project. The source agreement identifies the records, permitted uses, term and source price. HESTIA then signs a separate licence with the buyer.

Can buyers or their customers contact the source directly?

Buyers and their customers work exclusively through HESTIA for rights verification, technical questions, contracting, delivery and payment. HESTIA accepts work that can be completed without the buyer or its customers contacting the source directly.

How is each project priced?

HESTIA prices each project separately. The quote depends on the requested records, scope of rights, permitted uses, any preparation or review work, delivery and acceptance criteria. The written quote sets the price and payment terms for that project.

Can the records be used for AI training or evaluation?

AI training, evaluation, redistribution and third-party access depend on the uses and users listed in the buyer licence and on the written rights HESTIA receives from the source. Any right for HESTIA to retain a copy, license the records to another buyer or develop derived products is agreed separately.

Are the example records available to buy today?

The examples describe what buyers can request. HESTIA confirms availability and licensing rights for each project before making an offer.

What should a buyer include in a request?

Describe one project or facility, the documents and formats you need, the language, approximate volume, intended uses, acceptance criteria and indicative budget. Explain any required links between records, such as a drawing, its approved revision and the matching acceptance record.

What should a public body or private rights holder include?

Describe one project, the record types, language and approximate volume. HESTIA can then discuss which records could be licensed, what you are authorised to license and which restrictions would apply.

When should I share confidential documents?

Keep project files, names of third parties and personal data out of your first enquiry. Agree the recipients, permissions and handling of confidential material before sharing it.
