What we collect

This website is informational. The brief form prepares an email draft on your device; it does not submit the brief to us. We receive your brief only when you send the email. We do not use visitor-identification scripts or advertising pixels. If you email us at info@segegroup.com, we process your address and message only to answer your enquiry.

Hosting logs

The site is served via GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, which process standard technical logs (IP address, browser, requested page) to deliver and secure the service.

External fonts and project data

The main page loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to download these files. This website notice covers browsing and email enquiries. Before any field or video collection project, the scope must separately define the data collected, permissions, permitted uses, access, retention and deletion arrangements. Do not send confidential files in an initial brief.

Your rights

Under GDPR and Turkish KVKK you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data by writing to info@segegroup.com.
