Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Hestia Data — An AI Work School company, Ankara, Türkiye

What we collect

The online brief form sends your name, work email, optional company and service selection, brief, request ID and recognised campaign labels to our Hestia intake service at ortak-akil.onrender.com. A server receipt confirms storage; it does not confirm project acceptance or email delivery. Brief records are available to separately authorised reviewers for 90 days from the first accepted submission. Keep confidential files out of the initial brief.

Email and copy remain separate manual options. Copying a brief or opening an email draft does not send it. If online delivery is unknown, keep the page open and follow its same-request retry instructions before choosing another route. We do not use visitor-identification scripts or advertising pixels. If you email us at info@segegroup.com, we process your address and message to answer your enquiry.

Hosting logs

The site is served via GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. The online brief service is hosted on Render in Oregon, United States. These providers process standard technical logs (IP address, browser, requested page) to deliver and secure their services.

External fonts and project data

The main page loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to download these files. This website notice covers browsing, online briefs and email enquiries. Before any field or video collection project, the scope must separately define the data collected, permissions, permitted uses, access, retention and deletion arrangements. Do not send confidential files in an initial brief.

Your rights

Under GDPR and Turkish KVKK you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data by writing to info@segegroup.com.
