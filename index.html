What teams ask before the first order

How do we pay?

The written quote names the legal contracting entity and confirms invoicing currency, taxes, payment method and milestones before work starts. This website does not accept payment or create an order.

Can we start under NDA?

Request an NDA before sharing confidential material. The required agreement, permitted recipients and handling arrangements must be settled before disclosure.

How do you handle personal data?

Recordings and location evidence can contain personal data even after masking. Before collection, agree necessary permissions, permitted uses, third-party handling, access, retention and deletion. See the website privacy notice for browsing and enquiry data.

What will you not do?

We scope record retrieval and permitted field observations. We do not provide a legal opinion, certified audit, supplier-performance guarantee or model-performance guarantee.

Who is behind HESTIA Data?

HESTIA Data is an Ankara-based service brand associated with SEGE Group and AI Work School. Your written quote and agreement must identify the legal entity responsible for the project, invoice and payment. A brand name alone is not the contracting party.
