01

Start with your brief

Choose the right service and know what happens next.

Which of the three service areas fits my project?

Egocentric household video is for first-person recordings of everyday tasks. Field services bring together location observations, photos and available records. Technical task pilots start with the skilled task and capture specification you define. A project can combine areas if each part is feasible and clearly scoped.

Who is HESTIA Data for?

AI and robotics teams can enquire about task recordings; research, procurement and operations teams can enquire about evidence from a location or supplier. Start with the decision or workflow the material needs to support. We review whether the requested work fits our scope before proposing a project.

What should I include in the first brief?

Share the service you need, task or research question, proposed region and site type, intended use, required outputs, target sample size and any timing constraints. For recordings, add your capture specification. If a detail is undecided, say so. Keep confidential files and personal data out of the initial enquiry.

What happens after I send a brief?

We review feasibility and ask about missing requirements. Before work begins, a written proposal needs to identify the responsible legal entity and delivery owner, scope, outputs, price, proposed dates, permissions and acceptance process. An enquiry or an email draft is not a confirmed booking or order.

Can I enquire before all the technical details are settled?

Yes. Explain the intended use, what you already know and what remains open. We can assess the request, but missing specifications, access or responsibilities must be resolved before collection is agreed. A requirement is not treated as a confirmed capability simply because it appears in a brief.

02

Plan a useful pilot

Define the task, people, environment and recording requirements.

Can we start with a small pilot or sample?

Request a limited batch with a clear task list, sample size and review criteria. Its scope, price and dates are agreed before recording. Review the result and any open issues before deciding whether to commission more work; a pilot does not commit either side to a larger programme.

Are ready-to-buy datasets available?

This site describes services; it does not promise a stock dataset available for immediate purchase. Any proposed existing material must be identified and inspected, with provenance, permitted uses and licensing confirmed before an order. For a custom collection, start with your task and delivery requirements.

What belongs in a recording specification?

Include task steps and variations, the viewpoint, environment and objects, recording conditions, required resolution and frame rate, metadata or annotation fields, excluded content and review criteria. List any sensor or synchronisation requirements. The pilot proposal must resolve which requirements are feasible and how the sample will be checked.

Can you deliver my format or use particular equipment?

Put formats such as MP4 with JSON, RLDS, HDF5 or LeRobot, and any pose or sensor requirements, in your brief. These are requirements to assess, not a list of guaranteed capabilities. Equipment availability, recording quality, conversion work and a sample your team can inspect must be confirmed before commitment.

How are suitable people and environments arranged?

We assess the task skills, suitable participants, workspace, objects, equipment and technical supervision needed for the proposed pilot. Availability, competence, access, participant permissions and safety responsibilities must be confirmed before recording. A service description does not mean a trained team or a ready site is already available in every location.

Can a dedicated fixture or physical setup be included?

A task-specific fixture or working setup can be considered as supporting work for an agreed pilot. Its design, fit, fabrication, required technical expertise, safety responsibilities, cost and review need a separate scope. It is not an automatic inclusion or an off-the-shelf product promise.

Do you provide industrial camera integration?

It is a conditional option, considered separately only for a concrete factory need, an identified budget and a suitably qualified technical partner. Technical feasibility, equipment, responsibilities and the proposed integration scope must be agreed before a commitment. It is not the default recording-pilot offer.

03

Rights, delivery & review

Know what the material can establish and how you will accept it.

Are the website examples completed customer projects?

No. The JSON manifest is an illustrative schema without footage; the sample report is fictional. They explain possible delivery structures, not past customer results, available inventory or proven equipment capability. Your own sample and deliverables are defined in the proposal.

How are permissions and personal data handled?

Before collection, agree participant and site permissions, necessary data, permitted uses, third-party handling, access, masking, retention and deletion. Video and location evidence can still contain personal data after masking. A project needs its own handling arrangements; a generic compliance or anonymisation guarantee is not a substitute. See the website privacy notice for browsing and enquiries.

Who owns the material, and can we use it to train a model?

Ownership or licence, training and other intended uses, commercial use, redistribution, exclusivity and third-party access must be agreed in writing, together with the necessary participant and source rights. Do not assume that every use or an unrestricted transfer of rights is included. Retention and deletion also belong in the agreement.

Can we work under an NDA?

Request an NDA before sharing confidential material. The parties, permitted recipients and handling arrangements need to be settled before disclosure. The first enquiry can describe the task and constraints without attaching private files, customer records or a confidential specification.

What will we receive, and what do we do with it next?

The proposal identifies the files, formats, metadata or source notes, review records and handover method required for your project. On delivery, your team checks the package against the agreed criteria, records any issues and confirms the next step. A larger batch, additional analysis or new location is a separate decision.

How are acceptance, rejection and rework agreed?

Before work starts, agree review criteria, tolerances, exclusions, the review window and how issues will be reported. The proposal must explain what qualifies for rework, who is responsible, any additional cost and how changes to the brief are handled. Rejected material, repeat visits and unlimited revisions are not automatically free or included.

What can the work not establish?

A field visit records permitted observations at a place and time; it does not prove every fact about a supplier. We do not offer a legal opinion, certified audit, access without permission, supplier-performance guarantee or model-performance guarantee. Specialist reviews or requirements outside the agreed scope need separate assessment.

04

Locations, price & contact

Confirm practical arrangements before committing to the work.

Can you work beyond Ankara, including Northern Cyprus and Georgia?

Coordination is based in Ankara. We assess project requests throughout Türkiye, Northern Cyprus and Georgia. Local access, permissions, technical needs, suitable people and environments, and logistics must be reviewed before we confirm feasibility, coverage or dates. These are regions for project enquiries, not a promise of a ready local team or unconditional delivery.

Do you have a local team everywhere, and how is travel handled?

Local staffing or a site is not assumed to be ready for every request. The proposal must identify the delivery arrangement and responsibilities, any required local expertise, site access, travel and logistics, and their effect on price and timing. A location is accepted only after those practical requirements have been checked.

Can we communicate and receive the handover in English?

An English brief, file descriptions and review notes can be agreed for the project. Tell us if interviews, local-language work, translation or specialist terminology are required. The language skills, translation scope and any specialist review must be confirmed separately; certified translation is not implied.

How are pricing, payment and the contracting entity confirmed?

Price depends on scope, locations, people, equipment, logistics, permissions, outputs and review work. The written quote names the legal entity responsible for the agreement, invoice and payment, and confirms currency, taxes, payment method and milestones. HESTIA Data is a service brand. This website does not take payment or create an order.

When can work start, and how long will it take?

Proposed dates depend on the task, location, access, permissions, suitable people, equipment, logistics and review requirements. We confirm the schedule in the written proposal after feasibility review. There is no blanket turnaround promise; changes to the brief or unresolved access can change the plan.

Email and copy are separate manual options; opening or copying an email draft does not confirm delivery. General enquiries can use info@segegroup.com.
