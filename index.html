What teams ask before the first order

How do we pay?

Invoices in USD or EUR, paid by bank transfer. Vendor forms and tax forms completed on request.

Can we start under NDA?

Mutual NDA on request before you share a brief or a supplier name.

How do you handle personal data?

Faces and licence plates are blurred by default. No personal data is collected unless your brief needs it with documented consent (KVKK / GDPR).

What will you not do?

We retrieve public records, verify addresses and capture field evidence. We do not serve court papers or give legal opinions.

Who is behind HESTIA Data?
